# Earlier updates (Oct 12 to Nov 2, 2026)

Summaries of the big updates before the story bank release, grouped by phase. Pull request numbers are in parentheses.

## Oct 12 to 14: the game takes shape (#1 to #20)
- Judge Sim rebuilt as a North Carolina Superior Court bench-trial simulator with a statute-based law library, TestFlight builds, and a version and build display.
- Court calendar, continuances, case review, ask-counsel-anything, appeals tracker, defendant status, full case files, case-driven testimony, and investigative continuances.
- Plea colloquy (Alford, no contest, withdrawal), motions to dismiss, right-to-testify advisement, the insanity verdict, grouped judgments, bench conference, and any lesser-included offense as a verdict.
- The Mahogany Bench theme: walnut palette, gold stage bar, red objection cards, a bottom action bar.
- First 48 written cases, and an audit of every generated case before it reaches the bench.

## Oct 15 to 19: written cases and a living world (#21 to #34)
- 73 written cases, post-release supervision, probation after active terms, per-judgment consecutive control, and defendant profiles that add up.
- Weekly court calendar, administrative docket, warrants, bond, news, reputation, supervision violations, court holidays, and a weekly trial cap.
- Stay-away orders, consequences when guilty people are let go, appeal grounds and Court of Appeals decisions, victim profiles, and real-looking exhibits.
- Every trial became a written case; generated trials were removed.

## Oct 20 to 23: realism and the county remembers (#35 to #48)
- 25 new cases, inline objections, realistic bank, phone, location and business records, and a separate Dismissed verdict.
- Court calendar tab with a month view, perjury referrals, alibi checks, and real receipts.
- Daily docket with your own court days, per-day limits, time off, urgency, and reassigning cases.
- A living county: aftermath beats, follow-ups, a daily paper, investigations, and consistent defendants.
- A world that remembers: victims and witnesses are permanent people, officers and lawyers keep track records, suppressed evidence follows the officer, and residents return on the stand.
- Violation hearings for probationers, and time-served reactions that no longer send released defendants to prison.

## Oct 25 to 29: bond, calendar call and incidents (#48 to #59)
- Calendar call keeps your trial dates, with realistic failures to appear and plea variety.
- Bond hearings, a growing backlog, local news, and a headless playtest harness with its fixes.
- Intake limit, contempt hearings, petitions (expunctions, bond forfeitures, driving privileges), treatment courts, courtroom incidents, and storms.
- Full story consistency review of all 184 written cases; orders for arrest play out; the community reacts to rulings.
- Holiday requests at calendar calls and bond hearings, a ruling log, and People search and archive.

## Oct 30 to Nov 2: corrections and the path from crime to trial (#60 to #82)
- A new app icon.
- Corrections tab: jail capacity, state prison bed waits, sheriff releases, county crime rate, and jail or prison by offense class.
- Every case now develops naturally: crime, investigation, arrest, jail, bond hearing, arraignment or plea, then trial. New careers start empty.
- Iryna's Law (hold without bond for violent felonies), bail bondsmen, an Out on bond list, a Wanted page, and bond reviews from the Jail tab.
- Jail roster status, timelines for every case and person with clock times, and bond notes that replace instead of piling up.
- A month-view date picker colored by room and readiness, motion to continue with a chosen new date, and rushed-trial consequences.
- Standing rules where rulings move the bar and public ratings day to day, with ways to earn standing back.
- More names (about 280 new), no repeated surnames across a playthrough, and calendar cards that are never cut off.
