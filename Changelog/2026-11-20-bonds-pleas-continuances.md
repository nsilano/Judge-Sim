# 2026-11-20 Bond and plea positions based on the law, varied continuances, and fixes

Start a new career after installing this build, and restart the app. Bond hearings and plea positions are set when each case is built, so cases already on your docket keep their old asks.

- **State bond asks follow the law and say why.** The ask starts from the county bond schedule for the charge's class (G.S. 15A-535) and adds for prior felonies, prior misdemeanor convictions, missed court dates and a violent charge (G.S. 15A-534). A clean-record, non-violent misdemeanor gets an unsecured bond; a violent misdemeanor is treated as at least a Class 1; a missed court date cites the statutory minimum (G.S. 15A-534(d1)); a violent felony cites Iryna's Law (S.L. 2025-93); a Class A felony asks to hold under G.S. 15A-533. The old coin flip between secured and unsecured is gone, and any prior conviction now counts, not just felonies.
- **Defense bond asks are in proportion and give reasons.** The defense answers the State's figure with what the client can actually post (cash or a bondsman), picks real facts (ties, job, no missed court, no record) and rebuts the State's own ground. It no longer asks for release to a relative against a six-figure bond.
- **State plea positions scale with severity.** The ask is scored from the offense class, felony and misdemeanor priors, violence, a repeat of the same offense and what the sentencing chart calls for, instead of a random roll. Split-sentence jail days and probation terms scale with the case, joint conditions fit the offense (anger management and no contact for violence, treatment for drugs, restitution for theft), and the State gives its reasons.
- **Defense plea positions argue.** The defense counters one notch below the State's ask, argues from the client's record and circumstances, and answers the State's stated ground.
- **Motions to continue have far more variety.** About 20 new State objections and about 20 new defense reasons, fitted to the case (victim, lab evidence, video, many witnesses, jailed or released). The State answers the defense's reason when it can (lab report, video, plea talks, new counsel) and remembers what it said lately so it doesn't repeat.
- **Calendar call day picker counts trials you already picked.** Two matters in one calendar call can no longer take the same trial day without the day showing as full and a warning.
- **"Back from leave" notice fixed.** Storm closures and out-of-county assignments no longer count as leave, so you only see it after leave you booked.
