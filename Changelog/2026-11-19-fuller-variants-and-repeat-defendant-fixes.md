# 2026-11-19 Fuller alternate tellings, a bigger follow-up pool, and repeat-defendant fixes

Start a new career after installing this build, and restart the app.

- 198 thin alternate tellings were rebuilt as full retellings with their own defense witness, closings, identification line, and "not done" gaps in the investigation. Takeover story contradictions were fixed.
- 15+ new general follow-ups (drugs, driving, theft, family, takeover) and wider follow-up pools, so a returning defendant's new charge varies far more. A fifth of follow-ups now draw a charge that fits the person's record and style.
- Repeat defendants: associates are arrested together, life events follow repeated arrests (job loss, housing, marriage, child services), people only come back for a related victim when the first case was about that person, and the share of trials with a returning defendant stays under about 27%.
- Timeline fixes: a new offense is dated after the person's last court date and any time served; a plea ending in probation no longer wipes out a sentence already being served; an arrest order is not served on someone already in jail; a defendant in custody no longer reads "out on bond"; follow-up arrest dates match the bond hearing; early-release follow-ups cite the right month; merged probation terms keep both dockets.
- News: the 5- and 9-appearance items, the sheriff's patrol item, and the year-in-review no longer repeat or post when there is nothing to report. Probation-violation hearings count for less toward being a chronic repeat defendant.
- Text: new-trial motions name the count the defendant was actually convicted of; "Former retired" and the withdrawal-motion jail line are fixed; under-23 defendants are no longer dental hygienists; a follow-up where the person took a new job changes the occupation shown.
