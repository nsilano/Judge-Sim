# 2026-11-22 Safer saves, natural testimony, realistic drunk-driving timelines, and a faster start

Restart the app after installing. Your career moves to the new save file by itself. Start a new career to see the faster opening and the rewritten testimony everywhere: cases already on your docket keep the text and dates they were built with.

- **The career is saved in a file, with backups.** It used to live in the phone's small-settings storage (UserDefaults), which iOS treats as invalid past 4 MB, and a career grows about 40-50 KB a week. It now lives in a file in the app's own storage, written atomically, with the last two copies beside it and a copy of the career from before each update. A copy that can't be read is set aside, never deleted, and the next one is tried. A career saved by the old build is picked up and moved on first launch.
- **Witnesses no longer say the defendant's full name in every line.** About 6,600 testimony answers and 3,340 questions across 47 story files were rewritten: the full name once, then he, she or him, the last name for the State's witnesses, and the first name for the defense's friends and relatives. 238 answers that had no verb ("Gerald Lozano pulling away from my grip") are full sentences now. Where a pronoun would be unclear (someone else in the sentence), the name stays.
- **Drunk-driving cases follow a real timeline.** Impaired driving, death by vehicle and driving on a revoked license after an impairment case are traffic stops, and the arrest is the same day: the stop, the arrest about 20 to 40 minutes later, then the booking. They used to read as a report, an investigation, and an arrest days or weeks later. A written story's later "charged" line no longer moves a drunk-driving arrest. A chase where the driver gets away keeps its authored delay.
- **Blood-draw warrants are in order.** The stop and the arrest come first, the warrant for the blood sample is signed after, all the same day, as the affidavit says.
- **The first bond hearing comes sooner.** A crime has already happened the day before you are sworn in, crimes are more likely in the first two weeks, arrests in the first weeks come within days, and someone arrested overnight is at that morning's bond hearing instead of the next day's. The docket still starts empty and intake still ramps up from there.
- **A written story is credited back if you never read it.** A case you hand to another judge, or one whose defendant is never found, goes back on the list.
- **Roadmap and tools.** `ROADMAP.md` lists what is planned and not started (life after conviction, jury trials, hard calls, developing evidence, co-defendants). `Tools/Depth/names.py` is the testimony rewrite, run once.
