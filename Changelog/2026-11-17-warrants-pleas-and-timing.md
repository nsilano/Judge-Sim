# 2026-11-17 Warrants, plea options and timing

Start a new career after installing this build, and restart the app.

- More search warrant types: blood samples for impaired driving, bank and financial records, email and device contents, DNA cheek swabs, and firearm and clothing searches of a residence, alongside the existing drugs, storage unit and phone records. Each has its own affidavit and the same four flaws to catch.
- The length of a continuance now matters: given the full time, the ordered follow-up comes back; given about half or more, it comes back partly done; given less, it is not back when the case is called.
- At arraignment the State and the defense ask for different amounts of time, scaled to the severity of the case: a week or two for a misdemeanor, up to about six months for the worst felonies. Setting a trial date well short of the defense's ask can bring a defense objection.
- Small cases (misdemeanors) are now often offered a plea at arraignment, including a joint community-service deal. Every misdemeanor plea can also be resolved with house arrest and electronic monitoring, or community service with a fine and a curfew.
