# 2026-11-20 Consistent bond figures, varied lawyer reputations, and written stories no longer lost

Restart the app after installing this build. Bond asks and the lawyers on each case are set when a case is built, so a new career shows the new asks everywhere.

- **The defense's "most the client can pay" matches the card.** When no bondsman will write the bond, the defense now asks for exactly the cash the card says the defendant has, instead of a random share of it. A bondsman case says "through a bondsman's fee" when the figure is more than the client's cash, and no longer claims the client can post it in cash.
- **Lawyer reputations are spread across the county.** About a quarter of the prosecutors are known for asking the most the law allows, about a quarter for bargaining early, and the rest have no strong label. Defense lawyers get the same spread of "contests nearly everything" and "carries a full caseload and tends to take the offer". A reputation no longer changes when the roster does.
- **A written story is not used up if you never read it.** A case you hand to another judge, or one whose defendant is never found, now goes back on the list to be told again with new people, the way a plea already did.
