# Variants to rebuild as full cases

212 alternate tellings are thinner than the original stories they retell. A variant should be the same kind of case, a full trial, with the events reshuffled so it sounds and feels different from the original. These are short: about 2 exhibits, 4 questions, and a summary of roughly 80 characters, where the originals have about 4 exhibits, 10 questions, and a summary of about 240 characters.

## What a finished variant has
- A summary of the same length and detail as the original's (a few sentences, with specifics), told with different events, order and details.
- At least 3 State exhibits and 1 to 2 defense exhibits, each with detail.
- At least 8 questions of testimony across the State witness and the defendant (direct and cross), plus a defense witness where the original has one.
- A timeline of 4 or more entries, and openings and closings that fit the new telling.
- The same key, charge, victim flag, ages, victim fate and genders as the original (tokens only {D}, {DL}, {V}, {D.he/his/him/himself}, {V.he/his/him}, {DATE}).
